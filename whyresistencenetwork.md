# Why We Need a Resistance Network

**We need a Resistance Network so people can negotiate collectively over the value they bring to the digital economy.**

People depend on companies for wages. If AI reduces the demand for their work, that dependence could leave them with even less bargaining power. Companies, however, would still need customers, audiences, creators, and information about what people want.

The network would help people organize those contributions and set their own terms.

Imagine a million people agreeing that any app serving them must respect a few common rules.

- **My creations belong to me.** I decide who can distribute my videos and what they pay.
- **My data requires my permission.** I choose its purpose, price, and permitted duration of use.
- **My attention has a rate.** Companies can offer to buy my time, and I can decline.
- **My purchasing power can join with others.** We can ask companies to compete for our collective business.

A community registration service, independent apps, and automatic small payments would make those choices usable in everyday life. A company could earn money by providing a service, with its fees and permissions agreed upfront.

The network would also give people somewhere to move their activity when they reject a company’s terms. **The ability to refuse becomes economically meaningful when people have a usable alternative and can act together.**

Its community ownership would need to extend to decisions about membership, fees, disputes, and changes to the rules. Members would have the power to hold the network itself accountable.
